# YARDZ-
YARDZ is a construction equipment software built for contractors, equipment managers, and teams managing both owned and rented equipment. It offers a centralized platform for tracking assets, assigning tools to team members, and maintaining visibility across job sites, projects, and warehouse locations.
The platform combines essential operations into one system, including tool and asset assignments, location tracking, equipment transfers, and warehouse inventory management for parts and consumables. Through our construction maintenance software, teams can handle work orders, inspections, and maintenance tasks while keeping accurate records that strengthen accountability. Telematics data is also integrated into a single platform, providing users with a clearer understanding of equipment status and activity.

YARDZ also streamlines reporting by organizing project forecasts, fuel delivery records, asset utilization, and rent-versus-buy evaluations, eliminating the need to gather information from multiple systems. This enables teams to stay informed and make more confident decisions using consistent and reliable data.

Designed with ease of use, data security, and customer support in mind, Yardz enables companies to work across multiple systems and vendors without additional complexity. Its ability to manage both owned and rented assets within a single platform is a major benefit for teams looking for greater control and visibility.
